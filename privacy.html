Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy describes MiniDrive, a personal Windows and Android application maintained through the Dev-Master-Android GitHub account.

Google account and authorization

Google handles sign-in and consent. MiniDrive receives an account identifier (email address) and authorization tokens. It uses the account identifier to separate workspaces and queues on the device. MiniDrive does not receive your Google password.

What Drive data is used

MiniDrive reads file names, identifiers, parent folders, versions, sizes, timestamps, thumbnails and storage quota information. It uploads and downloads the files you choose, creates topic folders, and updates file names, parent folders or trash state when you request those actions.

The application requests the Google Drive read/write scope. This permission technically covers your Drive; MiniDrive's file operations are designed to work within the MiniDrive project folder you select. This scope is used to reconcile files you add or change directly in Google Drive, including files not originally uploaded by the app.

Where data is stored

Original synchronized files and application metadata (notes, captions, pins, chat state and revision events) are stored in your Google Drive. The app keeps local account state, preview cache, optional offline copies and copied pending-upload sources on your device. Windows authorization tokens are protected using Windows user data protection; Android authorization is provided through Google Play services.

Sharing and third parties

MiniDrive has no separate server that receives your files. Google processes Drive and authorization requests under its policies. Sharing or exporting an attachment is an action you initiate using the device's system tools. MiniDrive does not sell your data, send it to advertising services or use it to train AI models.

MiniDrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, cache and deletion

Files remain on your Google Drive until you remove them. File and chat deletion in MiniDrive uses Drive trash; you can restore them through Google Drive while they remain recoverable. Notes use synchronized deletion records. Clearing preview cache does not remove Drive files, pinned offline copies or pending-upload sources. Cancelling a queued new upload can remove its uploaded copy through Drive trash. Uninstalling or clearing Android app data removes local data and may discard unsent uploads.

Your choices

You may sign out in MiniDrive, revoke access in your Google account's third-party connections settings, clear device cache, remove local application data, or delete the MiniDrive folder through Google Drive. Signing out preserves the local queue for that account and stops its scheduled sending until you sign in again. Removing the Drive folder does not instruct the app to recreate it automatically.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact the maintainer through the MiniDrive GitHub issue tracker. Do not include tokens, passwords or private files in a public issue. Policy updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
