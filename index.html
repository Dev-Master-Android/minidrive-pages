Personal workspace

Your files.
One place to return to.

MiniDrive is a native Windows and Android app that organizes files and notes into topic chats using your own Google Drive as storage.

Ваши фотографии, документы и заметки — в отдельных чатах по темам. Один Google Диск, ваши устройства.

Each topic, a chat

Each chat corresponds to a folder inside MiniDrive on your Google Drive. Captions, pins and notes travel with your workspace.

Add now, send later

Files added without internet stay in a local queue. Upload resumes when connectivity and Google authorization are available.

Open and keep

Preview photos, documents and media. Cache helps reopen files; saving a copy to your Downloads or a chosen folder is an explicit action.

Google Drive access

You sign in through Google. MiniDrive requests Drive access to synchronize its folders and reconcile files you add, move, rename or remove directly in Google Drive. The app has no separate file-storage server. Your Google password is never entered in MiniDrive.

Private by design

MiniDrive does not sell your data or use your files to train AI models. Local previews, offline copies and pending uploads stay on your device; synchronized files and workspace metadata are stored in your Google Drive.
