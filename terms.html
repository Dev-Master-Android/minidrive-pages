Effective date: October 2, 2026

MiniDrive is a personal tool for organizing and transferring files through your own Google Drive. You control which account, project folder, files and devices you use.

Use of your account

Use only files and accounts you are authorized to access. Google services remain subject to Google's terms, storage quotas, availability and API limits. MiniDrive does not supply additional Google storage.

Local and synchronized actions

Pending uploads require available device space. Sending and reloading uncached content require internet and valid Google authorization. File renaming, moving, replacement and deletion may synchronize to your other devices. Keep independent backups of important data.

Development status

MiniDrive is under development. Availability, platform compatibility and uninterrupted synchronization are not guaranteed. This page does not grant permission to bypass Google security checks or account restrictions.

Privacy and support

Data practices are described in the Privacy Policy. Questions can be submitted to the MiniDrive GitHub issue tracker.
